Do I need any experience?+

No. Our foundation courses start with the essentials. We’ll talk about what you already know and help you choose a comfortable starting point.

What does a typical lesson look like?+

A short explanation, time to try it yourself, and feedback you can use. Expect conversations, exercises, and small projects — with plenty of room for questions.

Can I fit learning around work or study?+

Tell us what your week looks like. We’ll explain the available session times and practice commitments so you can decide what’s realistic before joining.

How do I find out about prices and availability?+

Start with a conversation. We’ll share the current course schedule, fees, and what’s included, with no obligation to book. Use the enquiry form below to tell us what interests you.
